Overview

The game

Unusual Grounds is a cozy café simulator where players prepare drinks for strange monster customers. These drinks vary from being a named recipe with defined instructions to being a completely custom drink where the player can choose any combination of ingredients, as long as it satisfies the customer's order.

My contribution

What I built

  • Customer spawning and customer archetypes.
  • Order generation and order validation.
  • Drink customization and drink-property tracking.
  • Customer patience and satisfaction systems.
  • Machine interaction architecture.
  • Day progression and end-of-day conditions.
  • Sound design and audio feedback.
  • UI and UX for order display, drink customization, and customer feedback.
  • 3D modeling for the café environment and characters.

Technical challenge

Designing reusable customer behavior

One issue that kept occuring during development was that customers would get stuck upon order completion. This was due to a flag being missed on the customer object that was responsible for removing the customer from the café. I solved this by creating an inheritance class that simplified the customer's variables and allowed for a more reusable customer class.
