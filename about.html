About me

A bit about me and what I like to make.

I'm a student game developer interested in gameplay programming, interactive systems, and designing mechanics that make players want to further engage with the game.

My primary development experience is in Unity and C#, with additional work in Unreal Engine, AR/XR, narrative systems, and web development. I also have experience in network architecture and cybersecurity, which informs my approach to building robust and secure systems.

When I'm not working on a project, I enjoy playing games, reading, baking, and paddleboarding on the lake.
