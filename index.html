About Me

Hello! I'm Teagan Foster, a passionate student game developer with a love for creating immersive and engaging experiences. With a background in computer science and a keen eye for design, I specialize in bringing ideas to life through interactive media. Whether it's crafting intricate gameplay mechanics or designing captivating worlds, I thrive on the challenge of pushing the boundaries of what's possible in gaming.

When I'm not coding or designing, you can find me exploring the latest trends in technology, playing video games, or indulging in my love for storytelling through various mediums.
